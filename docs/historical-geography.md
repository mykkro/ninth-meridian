The game's political reference year is **1453**, starting on 1 May. Quest characters and supernatural events remain fictional; the map uses modern GIS outlines rather than reconstructed medieval borders. Political briefs describe the 1453 situation and do not simulate every later succession during a long voyage.

* Toledo: Crown of Castile under John II. [Toledo Municipal Archives: medieval royal privileges](https://www.toledo.es/toledo-siempre/exposiciones-virtuales/los-signos-de-los-reyes-castellanos-en-los-privilegios-medievales-de-la-ciudad-de-toledo/).
* Amsterdam: County of Holland, held by Philip the Good since 1433, within the Burgundian Netherlands. [Dutch National Archives: Philip's register](https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/beleven/onderwijs/bronnenbox/een-register-voor-hertog-filips-1450).
* Copenhagen and Oslo: Denmark and Norway under Christian I. Stockholm and Österland: Sweden under Charles VIII. The Kalmar Union's crown was contested in 1453, so the 1430 description of a securely shared monarch is not applied. [Store norske leksikon: Kalmar Union](https://snl.no/Kalmarunionen).
* Turku / Åbo replaces Helsinki as the eastern Baltic destination. Turku is a medieval town and episcopal centre; Helsinki was founded in 1550. [City of Turku](https://www.turku.fi/en/current-news/planning-turku-800-jubilee-year-starts-last-weekend-january), [City of Helsinki](https://historia.hel.fi/en/kaannekohdat/origins-and-16th-century/1550-founding-helsinki).
* Novgorod: Novgorod Republic, before its annexation by Moscow. [University of Washington chronology](https://faculty.washington.edu/dwaugh/rus/novgorod/n-chron.html).
* Florence and Venice: republics. Bologna: papal sovereignty with extensive Bentivoglio power locally. [Oxford: Renaissance Italian governments](https://academic.oup.com/book/47598/chapter-abstract/422681736), [Bologna State Archives](https://archiviodistatobologna.it/i-fondi-in-ordine-storico-cronologico-e-per-tipologia/fondo/?r=IT-ASBO-CONTESTO-000002).
* Athens: Duchy of Athens under the Acciaioli, not a Byzantine-administered city. [Duchy of Athens history](https://www.worldhistory.org/Duchy_of_Athens/).
* Lviv: Ruthenian Voivodeship under the Polish Crown. The previously requested **Kyivan Rus / Kyjevská Rus** remains the broad story label; the political brief explicitly describes Lviv's actual jurisdiction rather than claiming a surviving Kyivan state in 1453. [Encyclopedia of Ukraine: Rus' voivodeship](https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pages%5CR%5CU%5CRushDAvoivodeship.htm).
* Bohemia and Hungary: Ladislaus the Posthumous; the Bohemian brief distinguishes George of Poděbrady's government and Ladislaus's forthcoming 1453 coronation. [Habsburg history](https://www.habsburger.net/en/persons/habsburg/ladislaus-postumus).

Modern country keys remain solely the geography used for country polygons, discovery, and transport. Per-location `realm` and `realmContext` values provide the historical state title and its bilingual brief. Language labels use familiar modern names for learning. The new city and realm crests remain original, stylized game art, rather than certified reconstructions of fifteenth-century arms.

Irish destinations use Irish names in both UI languages: **Gaillimh**, the established name of Galway ([Logainm](https://www.logainm.ie/69.aspx)), and three fictional sanctuaries: **Teampall na Fuinseoige** (Temple of the Ash), **Scrín na Taoide** (Shrine of the Tide), and **Mainistir na Naoi Réalta** (Abbey of the Nine Stars). The sanctuary names are authored Irish names for fictional sites, not claims of documented medieval locations. Internal location IDs remain unchanged.
