# Reputation

Reputation starts at 0 and ranges from −100 to 100 independently for each person, city, and country. Dialogue displays the current person's standing; the player panel displays local city and country standing.

Foolish replies cost 8 personal / 3 city / 1 country points. Insults cost 18 / 8 / 3. Harmful bargains or intimidation that cause a setback cost 25 / 12 / 5 instead. New quest conclusions and rewards grant 10 / 6 / 2; restitution grants 15 / 10 / 4. Replaying completed milestones or rewards grants nothing.

Cooperation uses personal reputation plus half city and one quarter country reputation. At −12 or below, requests for clues and other concessions are blocked. At −25 or below, normal conversation stops. Deliveries and restitution are exempt so they can repair trust; books are exempt.

When reputation is damaged, a player can spend a day helping the local community. This restores up to 10 points for every distrustful person present, 8 city points and 3 country points, capped at neutral. This fallback is available in every city, requires no resources, and cannot create positive reputation. The world retains completed quest progress and resets all reputation on a new voyage.

A donation to the local temple or church costs 20 copper and grants +4 city and +1 country reputation, with no personal reputation change. Each destination accepts one reputation-bearing donation per voyage. Donations support the local poor and do not consume a day, repair story setbacks, or erase personal grievances. A new voyage resets the donation limit.
