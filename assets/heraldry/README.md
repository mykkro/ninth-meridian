Original SVG heraldry for The Ninth Meridian. These decorative game emblems draw on heraldic motifs, rather than reproducing official or period-specific coats of arms. Country shields represent the fictional realms shown in the game; city shields use a smaller matching format. All artwork is local and requires no external image service.

The 47 shields use engraved double borders, metal edging, enamel shading, and detailed central figures. Country shields have a small crown; city shields have a masonry crest. Lions have faces, manes, claws, and shaped tails; eagles have individual feather lines; castles have masonry and portcullises; ships have rigging and carved hulls. The Irish sanctuaries have distinct ash-tree, tide-bell, and nine-star emblems. Details are authored within the same 100 × 116 viewBox so the crests remain legible in the existing country headings, city buttons, and map markers.

Florence, Venice, and the Papal States have separate realm shields. Novgorod uses a bear emblem distinct from the former Muscovite eagle. These remain stylized game motifs.

Turku replaces Helsinki as the medieval eastern port. Österland uses the Swedish realm shield. Holland and the Crown of Castile have their own realm shields.
